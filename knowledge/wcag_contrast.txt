WCAG contrast guidelines for accessibility:

Normal text (under 18pt, or under 14pt bold) must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background to meet WCAG AA.

Large text (18pt and above, or 14pt bold and above) must have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against its background.

UI components and graphical objects (like icon buttons, form field borders) must have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against adjacent colors.

Low-contrast text is one of the most common accessibility failures, especially light gray text on white backgrounds, or pastel text on light backgrounds.

Color should never be the only way to convey information (e.g. red text alone to indicate an error) since colorblind users may not perceive the difference.

Interactive elements need a visible focus state for keyboard navigation, not just a hover state.
